# JD to Interview All in One

A collection of three Codex skills for understanding a job description, researching the related product, and collecting real-world interview experiences from Xiaohongshu.

## Included skills

| Skill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`jd-quicklearn`](skills/jd-quicklearn/) | Turn an unfamiliar JD into a beginner-friendly role learning guide covering business context, responsibilities, knowledge, skills, and a learning path. It does not generate interview questions. |
| [`product-research`](skills/product-research/) | Research a software product, its history, competitors, business model, AI strategy, and defensibility. |
| [`interview-experience-rednotes`](skills/interview-experience-rednotes/) | Collect, screen, download, OCR, and organize public Xiaohongshu interview-experience posts into traceable Markdown, Word, and HTML archives. |

## Suggested workflow

1. Run `jd-quicklearn` to understand the role and department.
2. Run `product-research` for the products and competitors mentioned in the JD.
3. Run `interview-experience-rednotes` to collect source-linked interview experiences.

Each skill is self-contained under `skills/`. Read its `SKILL.md` before use.
